# Fun Surprises

Small quirks in Touchline's rules that a curious player can find and use. They are left in on purpose: they make the game more interesting to poke at. They are not bugs to fix.

**Rule for every phase:** when a review or playtest turns up a minor quirk that is fun or interesting for the player, keep it, add it here, and mention it in the end-of-phase summary. Only remove one if it breaks the game (crashes, corrupts a save, or makes every other choice pointless). If a later phase rewrites the code a quirk lives in, carry the quirk over or note here that it was retired and why.

| # | Surprise | How to use it | Where it lives | Since |
|---|----------|---------------|----------------|-------|
| 1 | **Free formation edge.** 4-3-3 gives +3% attack and 5-3-2 gives +3% defence at no cost. Attack and defence are averages over the players in those lines, so dropping a midfielder to add a forward or defender loses nothing. 3-5-2 and 4-5-1 pay for their extra midfielder with −3% defence and −3% attack respectively. AI clubs always play 4-4-2, so only you can use this. | Pick 4-3-3 when chasing goals, 5-3-2 when protecting a lead or facing a stronger side. It works mid-match too: change formation from the live view. Since Phase 3 a formation the team has not learnt yet costs up to 3% until it has played a few matches in it (Tactics → Formation familiarity), so the edge pays off once the shape is learnt. | `formationBonus()` in `src/lineup.js`, applied by `strength()` and by `power()` in `src/match.js` | Phase 1 |
| 2 | **Youth flipping.** Players aged 23 or under improve every week in training up to their hidden potential, and they are valued 30% higher than players aged 24–30. Buying a young player, holding him for a few seasons and selling just before he turns 24 reliably makes money. Since Phase 5, Hard training speeds it up. | Buy cheap 17–20-year-olds, train them hard, and list them at 23: clubs that would play them bid up to their asking price. | `trainWeek()` in `src/training.js` (free agents: `ageOneYear()` in `src/player.js`), `value()` in `src/player.js` | Phase 1 (kept 2026-11-15) |
| 3 | **Second chances.** A player you sell goes onto the free-agent list. Half the list carries over each season, so he can still be there a season later, a year older, and you can buy him back. Since Phase 6, your players whose contracts end go onto the list too. Since Phase 7 a player you sell joins the club that bought him (bid for him there to get him back). Only players whose contracts end, and players AI clubs release, reach the list. | Sell a player you regret selling? Check Transfers next season. | `endSeason()` in `src/season.js` | Phase 1b |
| 4 | **Retired in Phase 4.** **Idle-round income.** The Premier League's 38-round season ends while the 24-club EFL divisions play on to round 46. Premier League clubs keep collecting weekly income in those eight idle rounds (about €0.6M a season). | Gone: since Phase 4 weekly income is paid once per calendar week, the same for every club, whatever the number of matches. | `playDay()` in `src/season.js` | Phase 1b |
| 5 | **Free mid-match re-slotting.** Changing formation in the live view costs no substitution and can be done any number of times; it re-slots the players on the pitch to their best positions. | Switch shape freely during a match, e.g. to 4-3-3 when chasing a goal, or to re-slot after a red card. The new shape plays at its own familiarity (Phase 3). | `setFormation()` in `src/match.js` | Phase 2 |
| 6 | **Hard tackling has a price.** "Tackle harder" adds 2% to a player's defensive contribution. It costs more yellow cards, and since Phase 5 every fifth league yellow is a one-match ban (second yellow: one match; straight red: three). Kept as it is: the bans are the price. | Set it on defenders and holding midfielders. Switch it off for a player the Squad screen shows one yellow from a ban, or who is already on a yellow in a match. | `power()` and `foul()` in `src/match.js`; bans in `afterMatch()` in `src/squad.js` | Phase 3 (priced in Phase 5) |
| 7 | **Keeper up for a corner.** Now and then a goalkeeper ends up on the end of a corner and can even head it in, for any club. It is rare, a handful of times a season across all three divisions. | Nothing to do but enjoy the commentary when it happens. | corner branch of `shot()` in `src/match.js`; the keeper's `header` weight in `ROLES` in `src/tactics.js` | Phase 3 |
| 8 | **League One is a safe haven.** Nobody is relegated from the bottom division, because League Two is not in the game. A bad season there costs prize money, nothing more. | Take over a League One club and rebuild without fear of the drop. | `endSeason()` and `zone()` in `src/season.js` | Phase 4 |
| 9 | **Serve a league ban in the cup.** A ban counts down with every match the club plays, in any competition. A cup tie or a play-off can soak up a ban before a big league match. | Got a key player banned? Check Coming up: if a cup tie is next, he misses that instead of the league game. | `afterMatch()` in `src/squad.js` | Phase 5 |
| 10 | **Training finds the hidden potential.** An individual focus stops raising an attribute once it reaches the player's hidden potential, so a young player's Gained number stalls exactly at his ceiling. | Give a young player an individual focus on his best attribute. When it stops rising, you know his potential, and whether he is worth keeping. | `raise()` in `src/training.js` | Phase 5 |
| 11 | **Train your keeper as a striker.** Keepers train only goalkeeping with the team, but an individual focus works for anyone: give your goalkeeper heading or finishing and he slowly learns it. With fun surprise #7 he becomes a real threat at corners. | Pick a young keeper with potential to spare and set his individual focus to heading. | `raise()` in `src/training.js` | Phase 5 |
| 12 | **Two yellows can cost two matches.** A sending-off for two yellows bans a player for one match, but both yellows still count towards the five-yellow ban. If the second yellow was his fifth of the season, he misses two matches. | Watch the Squad screen's yellow warning: a player one yellow from a ban is the one to take off before he picks up a second. | `afterMatch()` in `src/squad.js` | Phase 5 |
| 13 | **Rest is free for an old squad.** Rest only stops players aged 23 or under from improving; veterans decline whatever the schedule. A squad with no young players loses nothing on Light + Rest and gets the fastest recovery and the fewest training injuries. | Managing a veteran side through a busy run of fixtures? Light + Rest. | `trainWeek()` and `recoverRate()` in `src/training.js` | Phase 5 |
| 14 | **Bans follow a player onto the free-agent list.** A ban only counts down when his club plays, so a player sold while banned is still banned if you buy him back. Injuries do heal on the list. | Selling a player with a long ban? Whoever signs him inherits it, and that includes you. | `passDays()` and `afterMatch()` in `src/squad.js` | Phase 5 |
| 15 | **Renew after a winning run.** A player's wage demand depends on his morale: unhappy players ask 25% more, happy ones (morale Good or better, 80+) 10% less, and never less than they earn now. Talk contracts after a good run of results, not after a losing streak. | Check the Contracts screen right after a few wins: the asks are lower. | `terms()` in `src/contracts.js` | Phase 6 |
| 16 | **The academy conveyor.** When contracts end and your squad drops below 16, the academy sends free 17-19-year-olds, and they are worth real money at a big club. Fill the squad with veterans on one-season deals, list the academy kids or quick-sell them, let the veterans' contracts end, and new kids arrive. It costs squad quality and is capped by squad size. | Running short of money at a Championship club? Sign cheap 33-year-olds for a season and list the kids or quick-sell them. | `expireContracts()` and `academy()` in `src/contracts.js` | Phase 6 |
| 17 | **Nine-tenths is enough.** Every club accepts a bid of 90% of its asking price at once, before any haggling. | Type in 90% of the asking price and skip the talks. Below that, a bid between 70% and 90% gets a counter halfway up. | `bid()` in `src/transfers.js` (`FLOOR`) | Phase 7 |
| 18 | **Fire sales.** A club in debt lists its two best-paid players, and both its debt and the listing take 15% off the asking price each, so they cost about 72% of the usual price. A regular who would normally refuse to drop a division will join you, because listed players go anywhere within one division. | Check the Transfer list after a bad run of results in the Championship: relegated big earners are often there. | `marketDay()`, `asking()` and `refusal()` in `src/market.js` | Phase 7 |
| 19 | **Players give themselves away.** Some true values leak through the fog: a free agent's wage demand comes from his true overall; "won't come" means he is a regular at his club; the Loan button only appears for non-regulars; and a club's counter-offer tells you its asking price. | Check a free agent's asking wage against others at the same estimate before paying to scout him. | `terms()` in `src/contracts.js`, `refusal()` and `loanable()` in `src/market.js`, `bid()` in `src/transfers.js` | Phase 8 |
| 20 | **Promotion makes them famous.** Fame follows the division: when a club goes up to the Premier League, everyone there becomes fully known overnight, and a relegated club's players go back into the fog. | Scout the Championship's promotion race late in the season, or just wait for the summer and see the promoted clubs for free. | `fogged()` in `src/scouting.js` | Phase 8 |
| 21 | **Staff on demand.** Staff levels can be changed at any time and are paid week by week, and Medical scales an injury when it happens. Pay for a top physio through the busy winter fixtures and drop back in the summer; cut coaching while you have no young players to develop. | Before a run of midweek fixtures, raise Medical; in the close season, drop everything to level 1 and pocket the saving. | `setStaff()` in `src/staff.js`, `staffBill()` in `src/finance.js` | Phase 9 |
| 22 | **Coaches judge their own.** The youth ceiling is a range whose width depends on coaching. Raise Coaching for a week to see the real stars among your youngsters, then drop it back before the bill bites. | Raise Coaching, open Youth, note the ceilings, set it back. | `ceilingRange()` in `src/youth.js` | Phase 9 |
| 23 | **A new job, a new academy.** Every job you take comes with a fresh youth intake at your new club, and the youth squad you leave behind is gone. Hop between jobs and you meet a new batch of youngsters each time. | A move in mid-season brings a second intake that year: open the new club's Youth tab straight away. | `takeClub()` in `src/season.js`, `hire()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10 |
| 24 | **The rescue job.** Every new manager starts with the board at 60 and cannot be sacked before six league games, however deep the crisis they inherit; that includes the summer check. The revolving door spins for AI clubs too: some sack twice in a season. | Out of work in spring? Take a job with fewer than six league games left: you cannot be sacked before the next season's sixth match. | `hire()`, `boardDay()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10 |
| 25 | **Ask at the right time.** The board answers a budget request by a fixed rule: yes when its confidence is 60 or more and the club is out of debt. There is no luck in it. | Check the meter on Board after a winning run, then ask. | `askBoard()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10 |
| 26 | **A new job, a new request.** The board hears one budget request a season, but the count starts again with every new job. Ask your old board, then take an offer and ask your new one. | Ask for more money just before accepting a job offer; ask again at your new club. | `hire()` and `askBoard()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10 |
| 27 | **Second chances.** A club that sacked you will not offer you its job in the same season, but a vacancy there later on, even one carried into the next season, can come back to you. | Sacked by a big club? Keep your reputation up and watch for its next vacancy. | `offer()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10 |
| 28 | **The golden handshake.** A sacked manager gets a quarter of a year's salary, and salaries are set by the club's size. A short, bad spell at a Premier League giant can pay more than a whole season in League One. | Take a big job you can't keep: the severance funds your next move. | `boardDay()` in `src/career.js` (`SEVERANCE`) | Phase 10b |
| 29 | **Salary is fixed at signing.** Your salary comes from the club's size on the day you take the job, so winning promotion doesn't raise it. Only a new job does. | Take the bigger club's offer even if it's mid-table: the salary jumps at once. | `hire()` and `salaryFor()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10b |
| 30 | **Buy low before promotion.** A club's share price is twice its expected income, which follows its division. Shares bought in a Championship club that goes up are worth much more the next summer. Sell, take the 5% cut, and buy the next riser. | Late in the season, buy a stake in the club you are about to take up, or in a rival running away with the league. | `clubValue()` in `src/finance.js`, `trade()` in `src/career.js` | Phase 10b |
| 31 | **Bring your own squad.** Players bought with your own money follow you to every new job, whatever that club's wage budget says. The board will grumble about the wage bill, but they come anyway, and you pocket the fee when you sell them. | Buy a couple of key players yourself before you move to a bigger job. | `bringOwn()` in `src/career.js`, `move()` in `src/market.js` | Phase 10b |
| 32 | **Shares outlive the job.** You keep your stakes and their dividends after you are sacked, and you can own part of a rival. A 25% stake in your own club makes the board say yes to every budget request. | Before asking for more money, buy up to 25% of your club. | `askBoard()` in `src/career.js`, `SHARE` in `src/finance.js` | Phase 10b |
| 33 | **Rest the veterans.** A personal Rest stops training injuries completely, and it costs a 32-year-old almost nothing: he barely improves anyway. | Put every player over 31 on Rest; train the young ones Hard. | `trainWeek()` in `src/training.js` | Phase 10b |
| 34 | **Buy the Golden Boot.** Goals travel with the player: sign the league's top scorer in January and, if he stays on top, your club collects the prize money and you the bonus, even if most of his goals came for someone else. | Check the Scorers table before the January window closes. | `ranking()` in `src/awards.js`, `payPrizes()` in `src/finance.js` | Phase 10d |
| 35 | **Change the division, change the race.** A striker sold to a club in another division takes his goals into that division's race, so a Championship scorer bought by a Premier League club can top a ranking he barely played in. | Buy the Championship's top scorer late in the season: his goals count in your division. | `ranking()` in `src/awards.js` | Phase 10d |
| 36 | **The retirement home.** Any star who retires from your club earns Star points, however short his stay. ## Design decisions that came out of this list

- **Free agents partly carry over (2026-11-15).** The free-agent list used to be fully regenerated each season. It is to keep half of the previous list (aged one year, retirees dropped) and top up with new players. Scheduled in `docs/superpowers/plans/2026-11-15-touchline-phase-1b-real-world.md`, Task 1.
